Q: How can I place an order online?
A: You can place an order by visiting furnitureco.com, browsing the product catalog, adding items to your cart, and proceeding to checkout by entering your shipping and payment details.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and cash on delivery (COD) for selected pin codes.

Q: Can I cancel or modify my order?
A: Yes, orders can be canceled or modified within 12 hours of placement. Contact us at support@furnitureco.com or call +91-98765-43210.

Q: Do you deliver across India?
A: Yes, we offer delivery to most pin codes in India. You can enter your pin code at checkout to confirm delivery availability.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days. Customized items may take 7 to 14 days, depending on the design and location.

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes, once your order is shipped, a tracking link will be sent via email or SMS.

Q: Are all online products available in-store?
A: Not all. Some products are online exclusives. Please call your nearest showroom to confirm availability of specific items.

Q: Do products come with a warranty?
A: Yes, all products include a 1-year manufacturing warranty. Warranty details can be found on individual product pages.

Q: Can I customize furniture?
A: Yes, customization is available for sofas, dining tables, and wardrobes. Visit a store or call customer care to discuss your preferences.

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for damaged or defective items. Please contact support immediately and share photos of the issue.

Q: How do refunds work?
A: Refunds are processed within 3 to 5 business days after inspection. It may take 7 to 10 days for the refund to reflect in your bank account.

Q: Can I view furniture before buying?
A: Absolutely. We encourage customers to visit any of our showrooms for a better look and feel of the products.

Q: Do you offer interior design consultations?
A: Yes, we provide free interior design consultations in-store and via video call. You can book an appointment at furnitureco.com/consult.

Q: How can I contact FurnitureCo?
A: You can reach us by email at support@furnitureco.com or by calling the store numbers listed above. We are available every day between 10 AM to 8 PM.
