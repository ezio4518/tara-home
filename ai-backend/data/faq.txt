-*- Frequently Asked Questions
-- Ordering & Payments
Q1: How can I place an order online?
A: Simply browse our catalog at furnitureco.com, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Follow the steps to enter shipping and payment details.

Q2: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and cash on delivery (COD) for select pin codes.

Q3: Can I cancel or modify my order?
A: Yes, you can cancel or modify your order within 12 hours of placing it. Contact support@furnitureco.com or call +91-98765-43210.

-- Shipping & Delivery
Q4: Do you offer delivery across India?
A: Yes, we offer delivery to most pin codes in India. Enter your pin code at checkout to confirm availability.

Q5: How long will it take to receive my order?
A: Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Customized products may take longer (7–14 days).

Q6: Can I track my order?
A: Yes, a tracking link will be sent via email/SMS once your order is shipped.

-- Product & Warranty
Q7: Are all products displayed online available in-store?
A: Not always. Some online products are exclusive. Call your nearest showroom to confirm availability.

Q8: Do you offer product warranties?
A: Yes, all products come with a 1-year manufacturing warranty. Details are available on each product page.

Q9: Can I request a furniture customization?
A: Yes, customization is available for sofas, dining tables, and wardrobes. Visit a store or call us for consultation.

-- Returns & Refunds
Q10: What is your return policy?
A: Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for damaged or defective products. Contact support immediately with photos.

Q11: How are refunds processed?
A: Refunds are initiated within 3–5 working days after product inspection. It may take up to 7–10 days to reflect in your account.

-- In-Store Services
Q12: Can I view the products before buying?
A: Yes, we recommend visiting one of our stores for a better look and feel before purchasing.

Q13: Do you offer interior design consultations?
A: Yes, free consultations are available in-store and via video call. Book your appointment at furnitureco.com/consult.
